Terms of Use

My Finances · Effective 22 November 2026 · Last updated 22 November 2026

Purpose: My Finances is a private, non-commercial desktop application used by its account holder to view and organise personal banking information. These terms describe its intended use and limitations; the application is not offered as a banking service to the public.

1. Personal use

My Finances is designed for the account holder's own authorised bank accounts. It is not intended for use to access another person's accounts without their permission, to provide account-aggregation services to third parties, or for commercial operation under the personal-use arrangement.

2. Bank access and consent

Connecting an account requires a valid authorisation through the bank and Enable Banking. The account holder is responsible for using legitimate credentials and granting only the permissions they intend. Access may expire, be revoked or become unavailable because of bank, regulatory or API changes. Each bank's and Enable Banking's applicable terms continue to govern their services.

3. Read-only application

My Finances is intended to retrieve account information and transaction history; it does not initiate payments or money transfers. It cannot replace the bank's official channels for banking operations.

4. Accuracy and availability

Balances, transaction descriptions, categorisation, reconciliation suggestions and financial summaries can be incomplete, delayed or incorrect because of source data limitations, synchronisation failures or software defects. The user should verify important figures with the relevant bank before relying on them for financial decisions. The software is provided for personal information management and is not financial, tax, investment or legal advice.

5. Local data and security

The user is responsible for securing their computer, application signing key, session information, financial database and backups. Sensitive credentials must not be shared or committed to a public source-code repository. Disk encryption, backups and access controls should be configured appropriate to the sensitivity of the data. See the Privacy Policy for additional details.

6. Availability and changes

No uptime, uninterrupted banking connectivity or error-free operation is promised. The application may change as it is developed. The use of Enable Banking and individual financial institutions remains subject to their own technical and contractual requirements.

7. Contact

Information about this personal project can be requested through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not share private banking information in public messages.
