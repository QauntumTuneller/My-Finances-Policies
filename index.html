My Finances

Information for a private, non-commercial desktop application.

My Finances is a local-first personal finance tracker for Windows. It is designed to retrieve account information and transactions, with the account holder's consent, through Enable Banking and present them in a consolidated view.

The desktop application is not hosted here. These pages provide information about privacy and use of the application.
