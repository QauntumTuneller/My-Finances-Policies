Privacy Policy

My Finances · Effective 22 November 2026 · Last updated 22 November 2026

Summary: My Finances is a private, non-commercial, local-first Windows desktop application. It is intended for the account holder to view and analyse their own bank account balances and transactions. Account information is retrieved through Enable Banking only following the account holder's bank authorisation. Financial records are stored on the computer running the application, not on this website.

1. Who operates the application

My Finances is a personal project maintained by the owner of the My Finances Policies repository. It is not a public banking or financial advisory service. Questions about this notice may be directed to the repository owner via their GitHub profile. Please do not post account numbers, credentials, or transaction details in public GitHub issues.

2. Information processed

When a bank connection is authorised, the desktop application may retrieve and store information supplied by the connected institution through Enable Banking, including:

  • Bank and account identifiers, account names, currencies and reported balances.
  • Transaction dates, amounts, descriptions, counterparties, references and status.
  • Connection, consent and synchronisation metadata, including session identifiers and expiry information.
  • Locally created categories, matching decisions, categorisation rules and other finance-tracking preferences.

The exact information available depends on the connected bank and the permissions granted.

3. How information is used

Information is processed to synchronise the account holder's data, retain transaction history, identify potential internal transfers, categorise spending and show account balances and financial summaries. My Finances is intended for read-only account information access; it does not initiate transfers or payments.

4. Banking connections and third parties

Authorisation and data retrieval are performed using Enable Banking and the relevant banking institution. The account holder approves access through the institution's authorisation process. Enable Banking and each bank process data under their own applicable notices and terms. My Finances does not need the account holder to enter their online banking password into the desktop application.

5. Storage and security

Banking data and preferences are intended to be stored locally in a SQLite database on the user's Windows computer. The application uses Windows DPAPI to protect imported application signing keys and supported sensitive connection secrets. DPAPI protection of credentials does not, by itself, encrypt the entire SQLite database. Full-disk encryption or Windows EFS may be used separately, where available. Local backups may also contain financial information and should be protected.

There is no developer-operated cloud storage or central analytics service as part of this personal setup. The application must contact Enable Banking and the connected banks to perform authorised synchronisation.

6. Retention and deletion

Historical account and transaction records may remain available after a bank connection expires or is disconnected, so that financial history is not lost. The user controls the local application files and backups. Removing the application alone might not remove its database; deletion of stored data requires deleting the relevant local database and any copies or backups. Revoking bank consent stops future authorised access but does not necessarily delete previously saved local records.

7. This website

These informational pages do not contain bank account data, login forms or application telemetry scripts. They are hosted using GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical access information in accordance with its privacy statement. This website is separate from the local desktop application's financial database.

8. Changes

This policy describes the intended personal deployment as of the date above. It should be updated if features, data storage, external services or operating practices change.
